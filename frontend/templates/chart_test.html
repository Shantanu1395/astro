📚 Chart Types Explained

🎯 Birth Chart (D1)

The main birth chart showing planetary positions at birth. This is the foundation chart used for general life analysis.

💕 Navamsa Chart (D9)

The marriage and spiritual chart. Shows deeper spiritual nature, marriage compatibility, and dharmic path. Each sign is divided into 9 parts.

💼 Dasamsa Chart (D10)

The career and profession chart. Provides detailed insights into career path, professional success, and work-related matters. Each sign is divided into 10 parts.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Dwadasamsa Chart (D12)

The parents and family chart. Shows relationship with parents, family dynamics, and ancestral influences. Each sign is divided into 12 parts.

🌍 Current Transits

Shows where planets are positioned right now in relation to your birth chart. Helps understand current influences and timing.
